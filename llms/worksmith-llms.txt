# Worksmith

> Worksmith is a retail operations platform (founded 2015, backed by Battery Ventures) that unites workplace and workforce management for multi-location retailers. Worksmith Workplace covers facilities management, vendor sourcing, maintenance, and work-order tracking through a curated nationwide network of vetted service providers; Worksmith Workforce (Progress Retail) covers scheduling, task management, training, and store audits. Worksmith is SOC 2 Type II compliant. Its platform API (api.worksmith.com, GraphQL) is private, with a "Worksmith API" component monitored on the public status page; there is no public developer program.
